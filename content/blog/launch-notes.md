The public repository has a narrow job.

It should explain what Nimloth Capital is trying to learn, publish the research that is safe to expose, and demonstrate an engineering standard that is easy to inspect. That means the repository has to be more than functional. It should also be readable.

The private data platform remains elsewhere. This site reaches into that platform only through a server-side integration layer. The browser gets a small read-only surface, not internal credentials and not direct access to infrastructure.
